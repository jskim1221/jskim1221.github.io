Applied Micro Data Lab

Applied Microeconomics · Econometrics · Data

The Applied Micro Data Lab (AMDL) is a research group at Sungkyunkwan University working on applied microeconomics, econometrics, and data-driven empirical research.

What we study

Research focus

01

People, Places & Policy

Housing, immigration, health, education, social capital, and regional labor markets.

02

Technology, Labor & Energy

Technology adoption, labor-market adjustment, energy use, and applied policy evaluation.

03

Networks & Econometrics

Social and financial networks, peer effects, spatial interactions, and empirical methods.
